# Key Areas

Ongoing parts of your life or business — things that never really "finish."

Subfolders here represent domains you actively manage. These get created during onboarding based on what matters to you.

**Personal examples:** `health/`, `finances/`, `household/`, `career/`
**Business examples:** `marketing/`, `operations/`, `strategy/`, `people/`

**How to know it's a key area:** Ask "Does this have an end date?" If no, it's a key area.
