# Time Audit Classification Guide

Reference document for consistent activity classification during time audits.

---

## Work Type Taxonomy

### Analysis/Synthesis

**Definition:** Making sense of information, identifying patterns, drawing conclusions.

**AI Potential:** HIGH — This is where AI excels. Research, summarization, comparison.

**Examples:**
- Researching a company before an investment decision
- Reading and summarizing reports or articles
- Comparing options (vendors, tools, approaches)
- Analyzing financial data or metrics
- Due diligence research
- Market analysis

**AI Could Help By:**
- Gathering information faster
- Summarizing large documents
- Creating comparison matrices
- Identifying patterns in data
- Generating first-draft analyses

---

### Creation

**Definition:** Producing something new—writing, designing, building.

**AI Potential:** HIGH — AI is strong at first drafts and iteration.

**Examples:**
- Writing emails, proposals, or documents
- Creating presentations
- Drafting content (blog posts, newsletters, social)
- Designing visuals or decks
- Building spreadsheets or models
- Writing code or no-code automations

**AI Could Help By:**
- First drafts of any writing
- Structuring documents
- Generating outlines
- Iterating on content
- Creating templates
- Suggesting improvements

---

### Communication

**Definition:** Exchanging information with others.

**AI Potential:** MEDIUM — AI can draft, but context and relationship matter.

**Examples:**
- Email (reading and responding)
- Calls and video meetings
- Messaging (Slack, WhatsApp, etc.)
- Meetings where information is primarily being shared
- Updates and status reports

**AI Could Help By:**
- Drafting email responses
- Summarizing meeting notes
- Preparing talking points
- Following up on conversations
- Sorting and prioritizing messages

**AI Limitations:**
- Can't read tone/subtext in real-time
- Doesn't know relationship history
- May miss nuance in sensitive communications

---

### Coordination

**Definition:** Managing logistics, scheduling, organizing.

**AI Potential:** MEDIUM-HIGH — Much already automatable, but often still manual.

**Examples:**
- Scheduling meetings
- Planning events or trips
- Organizing files or information
- Delegating tasks
- Project management
- Following up on deliverables

**AI Could Help By:**
- Scheduling optimization
- Reminder systems
- Task tracking
- Preparation checklists
- Logistics research

**Note:** Much coordination work is already addressed by existing tools (Calendly, project management software). The opportunity is in the gaps.

---

### Decision-Making

**Definition:** Choosing between options where the choice has real consequences.

**AI Potential:** LOW — AI can inform, but shouldn't decide.

**Examples:**
- Investment decisions
- Hiring decisions
- Strategic direction choices
- Major purchases
- Partnership decisions
- Prioritization of competing opportunities

**AI Could Help By:**
- Gathering information for the decision
- Creating decision frameworks
- Listing pros/cons
- Identifying blind spots
- Scenario analysis

**AI Limitations:**
- Can't weigh your personal values
- Doesn't understand your risk tolerance
- Lacks accountability for outcomes
- Missing context about relationships and trust

---

### Relationship

**Definition:** Building trust, rapport, and connection with people.

**AI Potential:** LOW — This is fundamentally human.

**Examples:**
- Networking events and conversations
- 1:1 meetings focused on rapport
- Negotiations
- Conflict resolution
- Mentoring or being mentored
- Social time with colleagues or partners

**AI Could Help By:**
- Preparing background on people before meetings
- Suggesting talking points
- Following up after conversations
- Remembering personal details

**AI Limitations:**
- Cannot build trust
- Cannot read a room
- Cannot handle emotional nuance
- The value IS the human presence

---

### Execution

**Definition:** Doing the core work that directly creates value or makes money.

**AI Potential:** VARIES — Depends entirely on what the work is.

**Examples (varies by role):**
- For investor: Taking calls with founders, reviewing deals
- For consultant: Delivering client work
- For creator: Recording content, performing
- For operator: Running the business process
- For builder: Actually building the product

**Questions to Ask:**
- Is this the thing I'm uniquely qualified to do?
- Is this the thing that directly generates value?
- If someone else did this, would it matter?

---

## The Leverage Question

For every activity, ask: **"Am I the bottleneck here?"**

| If the bottleneck is... | Then... |
|------------------------|---------|
| Information gathering | AI can help significantly |
| Synthesis/analysis | AI can help significantly |
| Writing/drafting | AI can help significantly |
| My judgment | Only I can do this |
| My relationships | Only I can do this |
| My physical presence | Only I can do this |
| Waiting on others | Neither AI nor I can speed this up |

## Energy Level Guide

### High Energy
- Sharp, focused, creative
- Good for: Complex analysis, important writing, strategic thinking, difficult conversations

### Medium Energy
- Functional, steady, capable
- Good for: Routine tasks, email, calls, coordination

### Low Energy
- Depleted, distracted, sluggish
- Good for: Simple tasks, reading, planning tomorrow
- Bad for: Important decisions, creative work

**Pattern to look for:** Are you doing high-leverage work during high-energy times? Or wasting peak energy on low-value tasks?

---

## Reactive vs. Initiated

### Reactive
- Someone else triggered this activity
- Examples: Responding to an email, answering a call, handling an urgent request, attending a meeting you were invited to

### Initiated
- You chose to do this
- Examples: Proactive outreach, scheduled deep work, strategic planning, self-directed research

**Pattern to look for:** What percentage of your time is reactive? High reactivity often means you're serving others' priorities, not your own.

## AI Potential Assessment

### Yes (High Potential)
- The task is primarily information-based
- Quality matters but not personal touch
- It's repetitive or follows a pattern
- The output is draft-able

### Partially (Medium Potential)
- AI could do part of it, but human judgment needed
- AI could prepare/research, human delivers
- AI could draft, human refines significantly

### No (Low Potential)
- Value comes from human presence
- Requires real-time judgment
- Relationship-dependent
- Highly contextual in ways AI can't access

## Common Misclassifications

| Activity | Often Classified As | Should Be |
|----------|---------------------|-----------|
| "Checking email" | Communication | Could be Analysis (reading reports), Coordination (scheduling), or Communication (actual correspondence) |
| "Meetings" | Communication | Could be Decision-Making, Relationship, or Coordination—depends on meeting purpose |
| "Research" | Analysis | Usually correct, but if it's procrastination-research, note that |
| "Planning" | Coordination | Could be Decision-Making if strategic, Coordination if logistical |
| "Networking" | Relationship | Correct if rapport-focused; Communication if primarily info exchange |
